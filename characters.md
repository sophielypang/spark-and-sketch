# Spark and Sketch characters

Names are not final. "Working name" means a name being considered.

## The Architect
- Hoodie: beaver
- White, female
- Likes building new spaces
- Personality: organized, curious, resilient, adaptable

## The Coder / Computer Scientist
- Hoodie: python
- Latina, female
- Likes coming up with creative solutions; thinks in systems
- Personality: quiet, easily stressed, persistent, likes routine

## The Physicist (working name: Naomi)
- Hoodie: dolphin
- Black, female
- Likes explaining how things work
- Personality: headstrong, methodical, precise, outgoing
- Always the one asking questions: "Why does this do this?"

## The Aerospace Engineer (working name: Carmen)
- Hoodie: falcon
- Asian, female
- Wheelchair user, but still very fast
- Likes aerodynamics and flight; tosses things to see if they can fly
- Personality: observant, adventurous, daring

## The Mathematician (working name: Blaze)
- Hoodie: spider
- Arab, male
- Likes optimization through numbers and patterns; sees symmetry, spirals and probabilities in everyday life
- Personality: curious, calm, interactive

## The Mechanical Engineer (working name: Harlo)
- Hoodie: octopus
- Black, male
- Likes tinkering and machinery; hands-on
- Personality: patient, problem-solver, pioneer

## The Environmental Engineer / Environmental Scientist
- Hoodie: bee
- Asian, male
- Likes thinking about how human invention affects nature and the environment
- Personality: playful, curious, enthusiastic
- Asks "How will this affect everything around it?" and looks at the surrounding environment

## Lesson hosts
- Mirror Magic (symmetry): the Mathematician
- Honeycomb Hunt (tiling, angles, least material): the Environmental Engineer
- The Trinket Box (nets of a cube): the Architect
- Robot Painter (sequencing, debugging): the Coder
- Ramp Racer (energy, friction): the Physicist
- Paper Plane Lab (lift, angle, nose weight): the Aerospace Engineer
- Gear Garage (gear direction and ratios): the Mechanical Engineer
