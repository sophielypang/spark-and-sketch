About

Why we teach STEM through art

Spark and Sketch is a nonprofit that teaches elementary-age kids science, technology, engineering and math through art. When kids draw, cut, fold and build, big ideas like symmetry and angles become something they can hold in their hands.

What we believe

  • Every kid can be a scientist or an engineer, and should see someone like them doing it
  • Making something is the best way to understand it
  • Lessons should be free, always

How it works

Each lesson starts with a short video hosted by one of the crew. Then kids learn the idea in a few steps, make an art project at home, and play a game that puts the idea to the test.

Money from the book and the shop pays for more free lessons and for supplies at our in-person events.
