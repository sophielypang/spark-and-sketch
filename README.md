# Spark and Sketch website

Website for Spark and Sketch, a nonprofit that teaches elementary-age kids STEM through art.

## What's here
- `index.html` — the whole site in one page: lessons with games, the cast, the book, events, the shop and an about section.
- `assets/character-sketches.jpg` — original character concept sketches.
- `characters.md` — written descriptions of the seven characters.

## Lessons so far
Each lesson has a video placeholder, the big idea in steps, an at-home art project, a note for grown-ups, and a game.
1. **Mirror Magic** (the Mathematician, spider) — symmetry. Game: paint the mirror half of a picture.
2. **Honeycomb Hunt** (the Environmental Engineer, bee) — tessellation, 360° angles, building with less material. Games: tile shapes around a point; pick the hive that uses the least wax.
3. **The Trinket Box** (the Architect, beaver) — nets of a cube. Game: fold or flop? (8 shapes; answers verified with a cube-rolling check, 11 of 35 hexominoes fold).
4. **Robot Painter** (the Coder, python) — sequencing and debugging. Game: program a robot to paint a pattern (3 levels).
5. **Ramp Racer** (the Physicist, dolphin) — stored energy, friction, fair tests. Game: pick ramp height and floor to stop in the zone.
6. **Paper Plane Lab** (the Aerospace Engineer, falcon) — lift, angle of attack, nose weight. Game: fly through the hoop.
7. **Gear Garage** (the Mechanical Engineer, octopus) — gear direction and ratios. Game: build a gear train to match a challenge.
